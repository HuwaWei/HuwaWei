## Fixed Income & Markets Research

- **[SGD vs MYR Rates: BNM MPC Trade Pitch](https://github.com/HuwaWei/sgs-mgs-rates-monitor)**
  Relative-value research on MGS and SGS curves into the Nov 2026 BNM MPC: curve bootstrap, carry/roll, FX-hedged cross-market value, scenario P&L and a 3-year historical backtest.

- **[Bank IRRBB and ALM Model](https://github.com/HuwaWei/bank-irrbb-alm-model)**
  Interest rate risk in the banking book for an illustrative Singapore bank: repricing gap, NII/EVE under MAS Notice 637 shocks, CASA behaviour and a sized SORA OIS hedge, written as an ALCO memo.

- **[SGD Fixed Income Model Portfolio](https://github.com/HuwaWei/sgd-fixed-income-portfolio)**
  Model portfolio vs an SGS benchmark: duration, curve and credit positioning, key-rate risk, ex-ante tracking error and scenario return attribution.
